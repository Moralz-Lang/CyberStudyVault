# GDPR HIPAA CCPA

### 1. Regulatory Frameworks
* **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** A comprehensive European Union privacy regulation that enforces data privacy rights for EU citizens, placing strict requirements on organizations that collect and process personal data.
* **CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act):** A United States privacy regulation that grants specific data privacy rights and consumer protections to residents of California.
* **HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act):** A United States federal law that establishes strict privacy and security standards to protect patient healthcare data and Protected Health Information (PHI).

### 2. Core Privacy Engineering Principles
* **Data Minimization:** A foundational privacy principle requiring organizations to limit data collection to the minimum amount of personal information strictly necessary to fulfill a specific, documented business purpose.
* **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** Grants individuals the legal right to request the complete deletion of their personal information from an organization's active processing systems and storage repositories.
* **Accountability:** A compliance requirement stating that organizations must implement formal policies, maintain detailed data processing logs, and conduct regular audits to demonstrate alignment with data protection regulations.
