

# ISO NIST COBIT

### 1. Framework Definitions
* **ISO 27001:** An international governance standard that outlines the mandatory criteria for building, operating, maintaining, and continually improving an **Information Security Management System (ISMS)**. It establishes a formal framework to align security strategies with broader business objectives.
* **ISO/IEC 27017:2015:** An extension standard that provides cloud-specific security guidelines and control implementation standards for both cloud service providers and cloud customers. This framework addresses security responsibilities and data protection measures as operations transition to cloud infrastructure.
* **NIST SP 800-53:** A comprehensive control framework developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology. It provides an extensive catalog of security and privacy controls designed to secure information systems against a wide range of operational risks.
* **COBIT (Control Objectives for Information and Related Technologies):** A comprehensive framework focused on enterprise IT governance and management, designed to align information systems strategies directly with corporate business goals.

### 2. Generally Accepted Privacy Principles (GAPP)
The core architecture for tracking privacy protections and governing data handling practices across enterprise systems scales across 10 defined operational criteria:
1.  **Management:** The organization defines, documents, and enforces its privacy policies and governance frameworks.
2.  **Notice:** The organization provides clear notification regarding its data collection practices, purposes, and privacy policies.
3.  **Choice & Consent:** Users are provided with clear choices and must grant explicit consent regarding the collection, use, and retention of their personal data.
4.  **Collection:** Data collection is strictly limited to information that is necessary for the specified purposes.
5.  **Use, Retention, and Disposal:** Personal data is processed only for its specified purpose, retained only as long as necessary, and securely destroyed when no longer required.
6.  **Access:** Individuals have the right to request access to, verify, and update their personal information stored in organization repositories.
7.  **Disclosure to 3rd Parties:** Personal information is shared with third-party entities only in accordance with established notice, consent, and regulatory rules.
8.  **Security for Privacy:** The organization implements technical and administrative security controls to protect personal data against unauthorized access or alteration.
9.  **Quality:** The organization maintains accurate, complete, and up-to-date records of personal data.
10. **Monitoring & Enforcement:** The organization establishes mechanisms to monitor compliance, handle privacy disputes, and enforce policy requirements.

### 3. Risk Management Principles
* **ISO 31000:** The international standard that establishes core principles, frameworks, and guidelines for managing organizational risk. It provides a structured approach to risk identification, assessment, and mitigation.
